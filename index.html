How your score is calculated

Your total is built the same way IRCC builds it, in three blocks:

  • Core factors: age, education, first and second language, plus Labour Market Integration. That last part counts either your Canadian work route or a job offer in a listed high-wage occupation, whichever is higher, capped at 150. The two never add together since November 3, 2026.
  • Skill transferability: combinations like education plus strong language, or foreign work plus Canadian work. Capped at 100.
  • Additional points: a provincial nomination (600), French ability, a sibling in Canada, Canadian study. Capped at 600.
